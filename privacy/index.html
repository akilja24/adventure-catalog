Privacy Policy: Can You Survive?

This policy explains what information the Can You Survive? Android app ("the app", "we") handles. The app has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email, location or contacts.

1. Information stored only on your device

Your game progress stays on your phone. That includes your current scene, the choices you made, scores, endings you discovered, badges, rank points and settings. We never receive it. Uninstalling the app, or using Settings → Reset all progress, deletes it.

3. Notifications

If you turn on New adventure alerts, the app checks the story catalogue about twice a day and shows a notification on your device. No push token or identifier is sent to us.

4. Children

Can You Survive? is intended for teens and adults (13+; some stories are rated 17+) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data collected by the Google services above is handled by Google under its own policies.

6. Your choices

  • Reset progress in the app, or uninstall it.
  • Change ad consent and reset your advertising ID.
  • Turn notifications off in the app or in Android settings.
  • Leave or delete your Play Games profile in the Play Games app.

7. Changes and contact

We will post any changes on this page. For questions, contact us at the developer email address shown on the app's Google Play listing.
